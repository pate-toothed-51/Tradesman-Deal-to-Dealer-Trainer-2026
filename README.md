## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **TRADESMAN: Deal to Dealer**. This trainer provides optional assistance with the game's trading, travel, quest, mercenary, and automatic combat systems. It is designed for players who want to experiment with profitable trade routes, reduce road danger, develop their party faster, or explore the game's quests with fewer resource limitations.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Travel between towns and villages with the merchant's cart. Cargo affects road danger, so use travel-related options carefully when carrying large quantities of goods or completing transport quests.

### Tools & Inventory

Trade goods occupy the cart's cargo inventory and have different traits that affect transportation. Buy stock where it is inexpensive, sell it where demand is higher, and enable inventory options only after the relevant goods are visible in the game.

### World & Environment

The journey begins in Grampstead and expands to locations such as Fishpool, Grubgrog Village, Hoboken, and Normingham. Use the trainer to support classic quests, plot quests, soulslike quests, and transport jobs without replacing the game's town-to-town trading loop.

### Combat & Weapons

Road encounters use automatic battles supported by active mercenary skills. The number and nature of carried goods influence enemy difficulty. Health, skill, and instant-victory options can make these battles more manageable while preserving the party-management structure.
